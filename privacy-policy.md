# Privacy Policy for Attendify Teacher

**Last Updated:** May 2026

---

## 1. Introduction
Attendify Teacher ("we", "our", or "us") provides a mobile application designed for teachers and school administrators to manage attendance and student academic records.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Attendify Teacher application.

By using the app, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

---

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to:
- Mobile application (Attendify Teacher)
- Associated web services (https://attendify.site)
- Backend infrastructure supporting data processing

---

## 3. Information We Collect

### 3.1 Account Information
When you log in or are registered by a school:
- Full name
- Email address
- Role (e.g., teacher, administrator)
- School affiliation

---

### 3.2 Student Information
Entered by authorized users:
- Student names
- Attendance records
- Academic scores and assessments

⚠️ Note: This data is controlled by the school institution.

---

### 3.3 Device & Technical Data
We may automatically collect:
- Device type and OS version
- App version
- IP address (approximate)
- Crash logs and diagnostics

---

### 3.4 Usage Data
We collect:
- Feature usage (attendance marking, score entry)
- Interaction timestamps
- Error reports

---

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing
We process data based on:
- Legitimate interest (school operations)
- Contractual necessity (service delivery)
- Consent (where applicable)

---

## 5. How We Use Information

We use data to:
- Authenticate users securely
- Enable attendance tracking
- Store and manage student academic records
- Synchronize data across devices and systems
- Improve system performance and reliability
- Provide support and troubleshooting

---

## 6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do **not sell personal data**.

We may share data:

### 6.1 Within Schools
- With authorized school administrators
- With assigned teachers

### 6.2 Service Providers
We use third-party services such as:
- Firebase Authentication
- Firebase Firestore / Realtime Database
- Cloud hosting providers

These providers process data on our behalf.

---

### 6.3 Legal Requirements
We may disclose data if required:
- By law or legal process
- To protect rights and safety

---

## 7. Data Security

We implement:
- HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
- Authentication controls
- Access restrictions based on user roles
- Secure cloud infrastructure

Despite this, no system is completely secure.

---

## 8. Data Retention

We retain data:
- As long as the school account is active
- As required for legal or operational purposes

Data may be deleted:
- Upon request by authorized school administrators
- After account termination

---

## 9. User Rights

Depending on jurisdiction, users may have rights to:
- Access their personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion
- Restrict processing

Requests should be made through the school administration or by contacting us.

---

## 10. Children's Data

Attendify Teacher is **not intended for direct use by children**.

Student data is entered and managed by authorized school staff. Schools are responsible for obtaining parental consent where required.

---

## 11. International Data Transfers

Data may be processed on servers located outside your country. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

---

## 12. Third-Party Services

We rely on services provided by:
- Google Firebase (Authentication, Database, Analytics)

We recommend reviewing their policies:
https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## 13. Cookies and Tracking
